Interactive Showcase

Premium Background Effects

A high-end catalog of beautifully animated background displays. Designed with custom shaders, dynamic canvases, and interactive scroll/hover parallax states.

Copying Showcase Snippets

1
Preview backgrounds: Hover or move your cursor inside the grid containers to trigger interactive depth, focus offsets, and distortion coordinates.
2
Get code panels: Click the "Get Code" drawer tab on any display card to toggle its source snippet container.
3
Inspect snippets: Click between the **HTML** and **CSS** tab buttons to view either the structural element markup or the synchronized style configurations.
4
One-click copying: Click the **"Copy"** button on the panel to copy the selection directly to your clipboard.
